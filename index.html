About Us

Allen Peace Oilfield Services Limited, operating as Allen Peace Energy Logistics Co, is a leading Nigerian oil and gas service provider specializing in upstream operations management and comprehensive energy logistics solutions. Led by experienced upstream operations professionals, we deliver integrated oilfield services that meet international standards while understanding the unique demands of Nigeria's energy landscape. Our commitment to safety, operational efficiency, and environmental responsibility positions us as a trusted partner for upstream operators, major oil companies, and independent producers operating in Nigerian territory. From drilling support to logistics coordination, we provide the technical expertise and local knowledge essential for successful energy operations.

Our Services

Upstream Operations Management

Comprehensive upstream operations support including drilling operations supervision, production optimization, well integrity management, and field development planning. Our team brings deep technical knowledge to maximize recovery while maintaining safety standards.

Energy Logistics Solutions

End-to-end logistics coordination for oilfield operations including supply chain management, equipment transportation, offshore crew logistics, materials handling, and warehousing services tailored to the Nigerian oil and gas sector.

Oilfield Technical Services

Specialized technical support covering well services, production facilities maintenance, pipeline integrity management, and downstream operations consulting. We provide hands-on expertise for both onshore and offshore operations.

HSE & Compliance Management

Robust health, safety, and environmental management systems aligned with Nigerian regulatory requirements and international best practices. We ensure full compliance with DPR regulations and industry standards.

Project Management & Consulting

Strategic project management services for capital projects, field development initiatives, and operational improvements. Our consulting services help operators optimize costs while maintaining operational excellence.
